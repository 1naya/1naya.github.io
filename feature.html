ABOUT THE PROJECT

关于这个项目 About the Project

一本名为《雷格纳斯漫游指南》的旅行杂志， 记录游戏里的风景和那些被人遗忘的角落，给未来留下一份纪念。

A small travel guide, documenting the game's landscapes and forgotten corners as a memento for the future.

拍拍风景，找些朋友一起留影。就这么简单。

Take some photos, invite some friends, and leave something behind.

一个小故事 A Small Story

这是一个以主角第一视角游历世界的故事。 一路记录风景，留下和其他人的照片。

This is a small story told from the protagonist's first-person perspective while traveling through the game world, documenting the places they visit and the people they meet along the way.

故事本身其实并不是重点。 只是我觉得单纯拍风景有点无聊，顺手加了一点故事，让照片之间有一些联系。

The story itself isn't really the point. I simply felt that taking scenery photos alone would be a little boring, so I added a small story to connect them.

SUBMISSIONS

投稿 Submissions

只要是在游戏地图里你觉得值得留下的地方，都可以投稿。 不论是风景、偶然遇到的地方，还是你自己喜欢的角落，都可以。

Any place in the game world that you feel is worth remembering is welcome. It can be a scenic spot, a random place you happened to find, or simply somewhere you like.

不一定要专门为了这个项目重新拍。以前拍过的照片也可以。 You don't need to take new photos specifically for this project. Older screenshots are welcome too.

投稿时请告诉我 Required Information

01 署名 / Credit 请留下你的名字或希望使用的署名。
Please provide your preferred name (Any name you like)
02 地点 / Location 请告诉我具体拍摄地点。
Please tell me the location where the photo was shot.
03 附言 / Message 可以写一段话，也可以什么都不写。
You can add a caption, or leave it blank.

地点可以截图小地图，也可以告诉我附近的传送点、地标或其他明显的参照物。 我需要根据地点整理这些照片。

For the location, you can send a screenshot of the minimap, tell me the nearest teleport point, landmark, or another clear reference. I need the location information to organize the photos properly.
